Upon entering the hostel, you’ll find a spacious area with tables and comfy sofas where you can rest, recharge, meet other travelers, work remotely, or play one of our board games.

Private Dorms

At Ocean Nomads, we understand that sometimes you need a little extra comfort and privacy while still enjoying the vibrant social atmosphere of a hostel. That’s why our private rooms are designed to offer the perfect balance between relaxation and connection.

Each room is thoughtfully arranged to be a cozy retreat, featuring comfortable beds, fresh linens, and a peaceful setting where you can unwind after a day of exploring the island. Whether you spend the day surfing, hiking, or simply soaking up the sun, you'll always have a quiet and comfortable space to come back to.

Perfect for solo travelers, couples, or friends, our private rooms provide the ideal space to rest, recharge, and make the most of your Tenerife adventure. Plus, as part of our community, you’ll still have access to our shared spaces, social events, and activities — giving you the best of both worlds: privacy when you need it and great company when you want it.

Come experience the best of comfort and connection at Ocean Nomads! 🌍✨

Shared Dorms

At Ocean Nomads, we believe that some of the best travel experiences come from meeting new people and sharing adventures.

Our shared dormitories are designed to bring travelers together while still offering a comfortable and relaxing space to rest.

Each dorm features cozy beds, fresh linens, personal storage, and a welcoming atmosphere where you can connect with like-minded explorers from around the world. Whether you're traveling solo or with friends, staying in a shared dorm is the perfect way to exchange stories, find new travel buddies, and make unforgettable memories.

You'll also have full access to our common areas, social events, and activities, making it easy to immerse yourself in the Ocean Nomads community. And after a day of surfing, hiking, or exploring Tenerife, you can return to a space that feels like home.

Stay, connect, and experience the true spirit of hostel life with us!
