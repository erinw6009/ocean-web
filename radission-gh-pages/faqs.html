Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We’ve got answers 🌊

What types of rooms do you offer?
We have both private and shared dorms to fit your travel style. Whether you want privacy or prefer meeting new people, we’ve got you covered.
Is Wi-Fi available?
Absolutely! Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hostel so you can stay connected 🌐.
Can I cook my own meals?
Yes! We have a fully equipped kitchen where you can prepare your own food and share meals with fellow travelers 🥘.
What activities do you organize?
From surfing and hiking to community dinners and game nights, there’s always something fun happening at Ocean Nomads 🌴.
Is there a place to store my belongings?
Yes, we provide secure lockers so you can safely store your belongings while you explore Tenerife 🔐.
