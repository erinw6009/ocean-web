Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We’ve got answers 🌊

How can I make a reservation?
Here in Ocean Nomads you can easily make a reservation through popular booking platforms like Booking.com, Hostelworld, or Expedia. However, to take full advantage of our exclusive discounts, we highly recommend booking directly with us here on our website.
Can I cancel my reservation?
The guest can cancel free of charge until 2 days before arrival. The guest will be charged the total price of the reservation if they cancel in the 2 days before arrival. If the guest doesn't show up, they will be charged the total price of the reservation.

We facilitate prepayment of the full amount from our guests before arrival, at the time of booking the accommodation.
What are the check-in timeframes?
Check-in: 14:00 pm to 21:00 pm
Check-out: 11:00 am
Can I do an early/late check-in?
Early: Yes, no problem. Reception opens at 8:00 am. The room won't be ready until 2:00 pm. However, you are welcome to arrive earlier and use the facilities at the hostel until your room is ready.

Late: Yes, of course! We can arrange a late check-in for you. For check-ins between 21:00 and 00:00, there is an additional fee of €10, and for check-ins between 00:00 and 02:00, the fee is €20. This charge helps us cover the cost of having someone return to the reception to assist with your check-in. Please let us know your expected arrival time in advance so we can make the necessary arrangements.
Is there a place to store my belongings after check out?
Yes, you need to check out by 11:00 AM. While we don't have a locked space for your belongings inside the hostel, we do offer a designated safe area on the terrace where you can leave your bags after check-out. This way, you can explore the area without carrying your luggage. Additionally, you're welcome to use our facilities until 6:00 PM. Please feel free to ask at reception for more details.
Can under 18's stay at the hostel?
Unfortunately, we cannot accommodate guests who are minors. Our policy requires all guests to be at least 18 years old to stay at the hostel. We appreciate your understanding.
Can I bring my pet?
Unfortunately, for hygiene reasons, we do not allow pets at the hostel. We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your understanding.

Ocean Nomads Hostel is an adults-only co-living and co-working space by the beach. A place to connect, create, and share adventures in Tenerife.
